What the buyer said, and why — verbatim

The same lines the terminal printed, in the same order. Reasoning first, then the move.

Before I opened a single conversation, a human approved what I am allowed to agree to. That brief is my wicket. The room sees it here because you are the audience; no supplier ever will.
wicket hard 29 units - ceiling 50.00/unit - net30 - lead <= 14d
wicket aim open at 60% of quote, climb toward 43.00/unit, max 3 rounds

That digest is what gets sealed into the purchase order — not the ceiling, not the target. A stranger can later prove I stayed inside a mandate without ever learning what my ceiling was.
seller Frobozz Supply Incumbent widget supplier. Regional warehouse, own freight desk.
seller Globex Corp National distributor. Container-scale pricing, port-side lead times.
One brief, two suppliers, same wording to both — if the quotes differ, it is the sellers that differ, not the ask.
→To Frobozz Supply and Globex Corp: RFQ-7777 — 29 x widget, net30, delivered inside 14 days. Quote me.
←Frobozz Supply: 52.00/unit net15 12d lead (1508.00 total)
"List is $52.00 a unit, net15, twelve days out. No volume break at 29."
←Globex Corp: 48.00/unit net30 21d lead (1392.00 total)
"$48.00 a unit, net30 from the start, but I'm three weeks out."
Globex Corp is the cheaper opener at 48.00 but is 21 days out, which is outside the brief. Neither opener is close to where I am told to land. I counter both — every offer gets a counter.
Anchoring at 60% of each seller's own quote. Not an insult — a probe: how far a supplier moves off its opener tells me where its floor actually is.
→To Frobozz Supply: countering at 31.20/unit (60% of their 52.00) to anchor low.
←Frobozz Supply: 47.50/unit net30 12d lead (1377.50 total) (moved 4.50/unit)
"$47.50 and I'll move you to net30 — the terms cost me less than the price does."
→To Globex Corp: countering at 28.80/unit (60% of their 48.00) to anchor low.
←Globex Corp: 46.00/unit net30 16d lead (1334.00 total) (moved 2.00/unit)
"$46.00. I can pull it to sixteen days; below that I'd be paying air freight."
Frobozz Supply moved 4.50 a unit off its opener; Globex Corp moved 2.00. The one that moved further has further left to move. One more round, then I stop.
Climbing to 40.00/unit with both — deliberately short of where I am told to land, so I still have a move in hand. Same number to each, and each of them knows there is another quote on my desk.
→To Frobozz Supply: 40.00/unit, net30, inside 14 days. I am comparing you against another quote.
←Frobozz Supply: 42.00/unit net30 10d lead (1218.00 total) [best and final]
"$42.00, net30, ten days, and I absorb the expedited freight. That is best and final."
→To Globex Corp: 40.00/unit, net30, inside 14 days. I am comparing you against another quote.
←Globex Corp: 41.50/unit net30 21d lead (1203.50 total) [best and final]
"$41.50 — I'll beat anyone on price if you can take the three-week lead. That's the trade."
Both are done moving. Now the part that is not haggling: does each final offer fit the brief my principal approved? That brief is private — the grading is not.
wicket Frobozz Supply ELIGIBLE
wicket Globex Corp OUT — fails lead_time
Globex Corp is the cheapest number on the table — 14.50 cheaper across the order. It is also outside a hard invariant, and a hard invariant is not mine to trade. The lowest price is not the winning price.
Frobozz Supply at 42.00/unit clears every invariant and beats the number I was told to land on. Over-negotiating a winning offer is how you lose it. I stop here.
→To Frobozz Supply: selected. 29 units at 42.00, net30, 10 days — 1218.00 total. Raising the PO.
→To Globex Corp: not this time — a constraint in my brief, not your price. I am not telling them which one.

What this run did NOT establish

  • The negotiation is scripted. Fixed ladders, no model, no live counterparties. It shows the shape of a haggle, not that one happened.
  • The offers and counters are not sealed as capsules. What is sealed is the digest of the negotiation record and of the winning offer, so an altered transcript stops matching — but a negotiation nobody wrote down leaves no trace either way.
  • No identity binding. No agent card, no JWKS URL, no DNS record. key_id → operator is bound only by this bundle's parties.json. The producer signature proves the holder of a key sealed a record, never who that key belongs to.
  • The AAuth Authorization Server is a stub in this process. Its Ed25519 key is real and its JWS genuinely verifies; nothing about it was deployed, discovered or operated.
  • No human was prompted at runtime, on either the consent review or the $125,000 refusal. The capsule records a decision a human made.
  • Integrity, not completeness. Nothing here proves a party did not simply decline to write a record down.
  • Nothing here is a standard. draft-rosenberg-oauth-aauth-01, draft-mih-scitt-agent-action-capsule-04 and draft-mih-agent-bilateral-attestation-00 are individual Internet-Drafts, work in progress, not WG-adopted.
